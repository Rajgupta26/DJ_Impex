Not all lace fabrics are created equal. Identifying premium lace requires attention to a few key details.

## Start with the base material

High-quality lace often uses cotton or blended fabrics that feel soft yet durable.

## Look closely at the embroidery

Fine, consistent patterns indicate superior craftsmanship.

## Feel the weight

Premium lace should feel substantial without being too heavy. Poor-quality lace often feels stiff or overly synthetic.

## A modern example

Modern luxury options like Zürique focus on combining high-grade cotton with intricate viscose embroidery, offering both comfort and visual appeal.

Choosing the right lace ensures that the final outfit not only looks premium but also lasts over time.
