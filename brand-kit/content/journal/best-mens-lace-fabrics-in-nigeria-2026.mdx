Men's lace fabrics have become a defining element of modern African fashion, particularly in iconic styles such as senator wear and agbada. In Nigeria, lace is highly regarded for its rich texture, breathability and inherently luxurious appeal, making it a preferred choice among designers and discerning wearers alike.

## The main categories

Among the most recognised categories are Austrian lace, Swiss lace and finely crafted cotton-based embroidered lace, each valued for its ability to combine structure with elegance.

In recent years, contemporary interpretations such as Zürique by Nabeen have emerged, offering Swiss-inspired lace thoughtfully designed for African tailoring. Crafted on premium cotton bases with refined viscose embroidery, these fabrics bring together sophistication, comfort and durability.

## What to check before you buy

When selecting lace, attention to detail is essential. Thread quality, embroidery finesse and fabric weight significantly influence the overall look and feel.

High-quality viscose embroidery offers depth, richness and a soft, elegant finish, whereas lower-grade alternatives such as polyester blends often appear overly shiny, coarse and less durable.

Ultimately, premium lace not only elevates the final garment but also ensures lasting comfort, making it a true investment in style.
