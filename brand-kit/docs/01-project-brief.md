# 01 · Project brief

## In one line
A premium, mobile-first **portfolio website** for Nabeen®, the luxury fabric brand of D J Impex & Co. (DJI). It presents the brand, its fabrics and its social cause, and turns visitors into **WhatsApp, phone and enquiry-form leads**.

**It is NOT an e-commerce site.** No cart, no prices, no checkout, no accounts, no payments. (Client instruction, stated in capitals.)

## Who is involved
- **Client:** D J Impex & Co. (DJI), Mangaldas Market, Mumbai.
- **Agency contact:** Binoli Shah (sent the brief and assets).
- **Build team:** Raj Gupta and Akky.
- **Replaces:** djimpex.in (currently an expired websites.co.in page) and the pitch prototype at djimpex.vercel.app.

## The client
- Founded **1995** in Mumbai. Covers sourcing, manufacturing, supplying and trading of fabrics.
- **Star Export House** status from the Government of India. Make in India.
- **Nabeen®** is its registered brand, exported to **Africa and the Middle East**, strongest in **Kano, Nigeria** (over a decade in the market).
- **1000+ designs.** Fabrics include Giza cotton, Swiss lace (Zürique), Atiku, Voile, Jacquard, Suiting, Wool.
- **Values:** honesty, integrity, human dignity; trust, quality, excellence.
- **Social cause:** a percentage of profits supports child education in Nigeria, including the **Wear2Care x Ali Nuhu** campaign with Ali Nuhu, the "Sarki" (King) of Kannywood.

## Audience
1. **Primary:** fabric wholesalers, distributors and retailers in Nigeria (Kano, Lagos, Abuja) and the Gulf (Dubai).
2. **Secondary:** tailors, designers and end customers who know the Nabeen name.
3. Most will arrive **on a phone**, from Instagram (@nabeen.ng, 10K followers) or a WhatsApp link, often on slower mobile networks. Design and performance must be mobile-first.

## Goals, in priority order
1. Look unmistakably **premium and trustworthy** (heritage since 1995, Star Export House, celebrity ambassador).
2. Make it **effortless to contact the team**, above all on WhatsApp.
3. **Show the range** visually.
4. Tell the **Wear2Care** story with dignity.
5. Earn search traffic through **The Fabric Journal** (lace, Swiss lace, men's fabrics in Nigeria).

## Success looks like
- A visitor on a mid-range Android phone in Kano can load the home page quickly, understand what Nabeen is in 5 seconds, and start a WhatsApp chat in one tap.
- Binoli and the client say it looks more premium than the prototype.
- Lighthouse mobile: Performance ≥ 90, Accessibility ≥ 95, SEO 100.

## Out of scope
Online shopping, prices, payments, user accounts, inventory, multi-language (possible later: Hausa / Arabic).
