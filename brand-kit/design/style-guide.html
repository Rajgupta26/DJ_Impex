Colour

Navy and white are the client's brand colours. Everything else supports them. Zari gold is a proposal and is used only as thread: never as a fill, never for text.

Type

One family, the brand's Open Sans, used as a variable font. Headlines use its condensed width at a light weight, which gives the tall, tailored silhouette. Body text stays at normal width for easy reading.
