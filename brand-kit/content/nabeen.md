## Introduction
Nabeen® is a renowned brand across the Middle East and Africa, crafted by D J Impex & Co. (DJI) to meet the flourishing needs and trends of African markets.

Nabeen embodies the core values of honesty, integrity and human dignity, with an ethical approach in all our engagements with our esteemed stakeholders. These values remain central to our identity and have played a vital role in building our reputation and success.
